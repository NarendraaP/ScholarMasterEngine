SUBJECT: Submission of Manuscript P2 — Probabilistic Multi-Modal Vector Fusion under Asymmetric Sensing Constraints

Dear Editor-in-Chief and Associate Editors,

We are pleased to submit our original research manuscript titled "Probabilistic Multi-Modal Vector Fusion under Asymmetric Sensing Constraints" for publication consideration in IEEE Transactions on Cybernetics.

1. SUBSYSTEM BACKGROUND AND MOTIVATION
In multi-sensory campus environments, camera occlusions, varying ambient light, and sensor dropouts introduce asymmetric spatial uncertainty. Standard sensor fusion models require aggregating raw video and audio streams at a central node, which violates privacy boundaries and incurs heavy bandwidth overhead. This paper addresses multi-modal reasoning strictly over anonymized vector representations.

2. CORE TECHNICAL CONTRIBUTIONS AND NOVELTY
  - Asymmetric Feature Space Fusion: Proposes a probabilistic vector fusion mechanism that integrates 512-dimensional facial embeddings, 17 keypoint pose skeletons, and acoustic spectral vectors without exchanging raw sensor pixels or audio waveforms.
  - Uncertainty Bounds & Low Latency: Demonstrates robust state estimation under severe sensor occlusion, maintaining high detection confidence while sustaining a vector fusion latency of less than 15ms.
  - Empirical Validation: Evaluates multi-modal telemetry across simulated and real-world campus event logs, establishing superior spatial tracking accuracy over single-modality baselines.

3. SUITABILITY FOR IEEE TRANSACTIONS ON CYBERNETICS
This work falls squarely within the scope of IEEE Transactions on Cybernetics, focusing on multi-modal information processing, probabilistic reasoning under uncertainty, and cybernetic control loops in complex sensing environments.

4. ETHICS AND ORIGINALITY DECLARATIONS
The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under submission elsewhere. All evaluations adhere strictly to structural data minimization constraints.
