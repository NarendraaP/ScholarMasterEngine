SUBJECT: Submission of Manuscript P21 — Formal Mathematical Foundations and Timed Automata Verification of Privacy Invariants

Dear Editor-in-Chief and Associate Editors,

We are pleased to submit our original research manuscript titled "Formal Mathematical Foundations and Timed Automata Verification of Privacy Invariants" for publication consideration in Formal Aspects of Computing.

1. MATHEMATICAL APEX AND SUBSYSTEM FORMALIZATION
Providing indisputable guarantees for real-time cyber-physical privacy architectures requires rigorous formal mathematical proofs beyond empirical testing. This paper presents the formal mathematical apex of the ScholarMaster systems-research ecosystem, establishing a unified axiom system and timed automata model checking framework for privacy-by-design systems.

2. CORE MATHEMATICAL CONTRIBUTIONS AND FORMALISMS
  - Timed Automata Model Checking: Formulates a formal timed automata specification governing the volatile memory lifecycle, proving that the probability of data persistence beyond a 33ms Time-to-Live (TTL) boundary is identically zero.
  - Axiomatic Invariant System: Establishes a formal axiom system and Hoare logic proof sketch demonstrating structural privacy compliance, fail-closed governance, and underdetermined image reconstruction.
  - Theorem-First Mathematical Rigor: Provides cold, formal mathematical proofs validating cross-layer isolation and runtime non-repudiation without relying on empirical heuristics.

3. SUITABILITY FOR FORMAL ASPECTS OF COMPUTING
This manuscript directly aligns with Formal Aspects of Computing's mission to publish rigorous formal methods, mathematical foundations, model checking proofs, and temporal logic verification of real-time systems.

4. ETHICS AND ORIGINALITY DECLARATIONS
This manuscript is an original formal work, has not been published previously, and is not under submission to any other publication venue.
