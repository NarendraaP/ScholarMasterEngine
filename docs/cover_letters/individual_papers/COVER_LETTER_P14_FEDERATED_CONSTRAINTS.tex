SUBJECT: Submission of Manuscript P14 — Hierarchical H-FedAvg across Multi-Institutional Campus Nodes

Dear Editor-in-Chief and Associate Editors,

We are pleased to submit our original research manuscript titled "Hierarchical H-FedAvg across Multi-Institutional Campus Nodes" for publication consideration in the IEEE Internet of Things Journal.

1. SUBSYSTEM BACKGROUND AND MOTIVATION
Scaling federated learning across multiple distinct university campuses introduces high network latency, heterogeneous edge hardware, and non-IID data distributions. Standard single-tier FedAvg incurs prohibitive communication overhead when scaling across geographically distributed campus nodes. This paper introduces Hierarchical Federated Averaging (H-FedAvg).

2. CORE TECHNICAL CONTRIBUTIONS AND NOVELTY
  - Hierarchical Two-Tier Aggregation: Implements a two-tiered FL aggregation hierarchy (Intra-Campus Edge Coordinator -> Inter-Campus Master Aggregator) that reduces WAN communication frequency by 5x.
  - Multi-Institution Gradient Privacy: Combines homomorphic gradient hashing with local differential noise injection to guarantee zero leakage of institutional data distributions during inter-campus aggregation.
  - Scalability Benchmarking: Validates multi-campus model convergence across 3 institutional deployments, demonstrating robust accuracy gains under non-IID student population distributions.

3. SUITABILITY FOR IEEE INTERNET OF THINGS JOURNAL
This manuscript directly aligns with IEEE IoT-J's focus on large-scale federated IoT learning, hierarchical network management, multi-institution edge intelligence, and distributed privacy algorithms.

4. ETHICS AND ORIGINALITY DECLARATIONS
This manuscript is an original submission, is not under review elsewhere, and has been approved by all contributing authors.
