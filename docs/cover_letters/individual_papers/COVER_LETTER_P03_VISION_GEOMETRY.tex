SUBJECT: Submission of Manuscript P3 — Pose-Only Architectural Irreversibility and Volatile Memory Confinement in Edge Sensing

Dear Editor-in-Chief and Associate Editors,

We are pleased to submit our original research manuscript titled "Pose-Only Architectural Irreversibility and Volatile Memory Confinement in Edge Sensing" for publication consideration in the IEEE Internet of Things Journal.

1. SUBSYSTEM BACKGROUND AND MOTIVATION
Deploying visual IoT sensors in sensitive educational spaces raises severe biometric privacy concerns. Traditional vision pipelines store continuous video streams or save high-resolution facial images in local storage, creating permanent leakage vulnerabilities. This paper introduces a hardware-isolated, pose-only sensing architecture that guarantees structural privacy compliance at the camera edge.

2. CORE TECHNICAL CONTRIBUTIONS AND NOVELTY
  - Volatile Memory Destruction Boundary: Implements a non-persistent RAM buffer engine that overwrites raw video frames within a strict 33ms Time-to-Live (TTL) window, preventing any write-to-disk of biometric image pixels.
  - Markerless Skeleton Feature Extraction: Extracts 17-point coordinate skeletons using a lightweight YOLOv8-pose estimator, discarding facial features entirely while preserving structural movement data for behavior monitoring.
  - Mathematical Irreversibility Guarantee: Proves that image pixel reconstruction from extracted keypoint coordinate matrices is mathematically underdetermined, satisfying GDPR Article 25 (Privacy by Design).

3. SUITABILITY FOR IEEE INTERNET OF THINGS JOURNAL
This paper directly aligns with the IEEE Internet of Things Journal's focus on edge-native sensing, IoT security, volatile resource management, and privacy-preserving vision frameworks for smart environments.

4. ETHICS AND ORIGINALITY DECLARATIONS
This manuscript represents original research, has not been published before, and is not under review elsewhere. All experimental trials were conducted under strict privacy minimization guidelines.
