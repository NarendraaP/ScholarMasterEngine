## 1. Summary of Verification Methodology

Every finding from the adversarial challenge was tested against the actual text, equations, and tables of the four manuscripts. Each issue was evaluated under the rule: **VERIFY FIRST. EDIT SECOND.**

### Verification Classification Definitions:
* **VALID**: The adversarial criticism is factually supported by the manuscript text; a hostile reviewer could exploit this vulnerability. Revision is mandatory.
* **PARTIALLY_VALID**: The underlying observation is true, but the manuscript already contains partial qualifications or the criticism exaggerated the issue. Revision is recommended to sharpen positioning.
* **NOT_SUPPORTED**: The manuscript already contains the required qualification or the adversarial criticism mischaracterized the text. No revision needed.
* **INCORRECT**: The adversarial criticism was scientifically or mathematically erroneous. No revision permitted.
